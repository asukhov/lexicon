About Lexicon

A self-contained English-to-English dictionary. Every word, definition, diagram, font, and interface asset is stored in this repository. There are no analytics, trackers, or third-party requests at runtime.

The Grammar tab is a twelve-topic learner reference with 103 rules, comparison tables and a tense map. The Illustrated tab contains fourteen original SVG diagrams whose parts link back into the dictionary.

How the data is made

Definitions and examples are assembled from open, learner-friendly sources, with WordNet supplying broad coverage and English Wiktionary supplying idioms, proverbs, and phrasal verbs. Pronunciation comes from the open IPA dictionary and CMU-derived data where available; rare headwords use an automatic eSpeak NG fallback, marked in the entry.
